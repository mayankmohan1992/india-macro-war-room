# The week in six numbers — 2026-W46

*Six Indian macro indicators, as published rather than forecast. Data as of 2026-11-08; note compiled 2026-11-13.*

Of the 38 indicators this project tracks, **19 are in range, 16 need watching and 10 are in stress** (rules are published with the data).

**The six.** 6 of 6 slots filled.

1. **Policy repo rate — 5.50%** (6 Nov 2026). +25 bp on 9 Oct 2026, from 5.25%; the benchmark for every other rate in the economy.
2. **CPI inflation — 6.09%** (31 Oct 2026). +0.55 pp on the previous print (30 Sep 2026); 2.09 pp above the 4% target, outside the 2–6% band.
3. **WPI inflation — 11.39%** (30 Sep 2026). +1.47 pp on the previous print; 5.30 pp above retail inflation.
4. **Liquidity (net LAF, 1-month avg) — −₹1.45 lakh crore** (8 Nov 2026). Absorption fell to ₹1.45 lakh crore from ₹2.57 lakh crore in 28 days; 1-month average of daily net RBI operations.
5. **Credit growth minus deposit growth — +1.51 pp** (15 Oct 2026). Credit 18.23% vs deposits 16.72% on the same fortnight; credit down 1.15 pp and deposits down 1.11 pp on the previous print; credit growing faster than deposits — banks fund the difference elsewhere.
6. **10-year G-sec yield — 7.21%** (6 Nov 2026). -0.19 pp over 28 days; what the market charges the government to borrow for ten years.

**Also · USD/INR — 94.14** (6 Nov 2026). −0.88 (−0.9%) in 28 days; rupees per US dollar, the rupee firmer on the month.

Nothing here is real-time: the oldest prints this note leans on are WPI inflation (44 days); Bank credit growth (29 days); CPI inflation (13 days).

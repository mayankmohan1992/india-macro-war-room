# The week in six numbers — 2026-W46

*Six Indian macro indicators, as published rather than forecast. Data as of 2026-11-01; note compiled 2026-11-10.*

Of the 38 indicators this project tracks, **12 are in range, 17 need watching and 9 are in stress** (rules are published with the data).

**The six.** 6 of 6 slots filled.

1. **Policy repo rate — 5.50%** (30 Oct 2026). +25 bp on 9 Oct 2026, from 5.25%; the benchmark every other rate is priced off.
2. **CPI inflation — 5.54%** (30 Sep 2026). +0.71 pp on the previous print (31 Aug 2026); 1.54 points above the 4% target, inside the 2–6% band.
3. **WPI inflation — 11.39%** (30 Sep 2026). +1.47 pp on the previous print; 5.85 points above retail inflation.
4. **Liquidity (net LAF, 1-month avg) — −₹1.44 lakh crore** (1 Nov 2026). Net absorption, ₹1.25 lakh crore less than 28 days ago (then ₹2.69 lakh crore); 1-month average of daily net RBI operations.
5. **Credit growth minus deposit growth — +1.51 pp** (15 Oct 2026). Credit 18.23% vs deposits 16.72% on the same fortnight; credit down 1.15 pp and deposits down 1.11 pp on the previous print; credit growing faster than deposits — banks fund the difference elsewhere.
6. **10-year G-sec yield — 7.45%** (30 Oct 2026). +0.08 pp over 28 days; what the market charges the government to borrow for ten years.

USD/INR at 95.25 on 30 Oct 2026, 1.36 lower than 28 days earlier (−1.4%) — a stronger rupee.

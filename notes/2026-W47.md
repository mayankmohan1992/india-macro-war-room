# The week in six numbers — 2026-W47

*Six Indian macro indicators, as published rather than forecast. Data as of 2026-11-15; note compiled 2026-11-20.*

Of the 38 indicators this project tracks, **20 are in range, 15 need watching and 10 are in stress** (rules are published with the data).

**The six.** 6 of 6 slots filled.

1. **Policy repo rate — 5.50%** (13 Nov 2026). +25 bp on 9 Oct 2026, from 5.25%; the benchmark for every other rate in the economy.
2. **CPI inflation — 6.09%** (31 Oct 2026). +0.55 pp on the previous print (30 Sep 2026); 2.09 pp above the 4% target, outside the 2–6% band.
3. **WPI inflation — 12.29%** (31 Oct 2026). +0.90 pp on the previous print; 6.20 pp above retail inflation.
4. **Liquidity (net LAF, 1-month avg) — −₹1.62 lakh crore** (15 Nov 2026). Absorption fell to ₹1.62 lakh crore from ₹2.00 lakh crore in 28 days; 1-month average of daily net RBI operations.
5. **Credit growth minus deposit growth — +2.68 pp** (31 Oct 2026). Credit 19.54% vs deposits 16.86% on the same fortnight; credit up 1.31 pp and deposits up 0.14 pp on the previous print; credit growing faster than deposits — banks fund the difference elsewhere.
6. **10-year G-sec yield — 7.34%** (13 Nov 2026). -0.18 pp over 28 days; what the market charges the government to borrow for ten years.

**Also · USD/INR — 94.20** (13 Nov 2026). −0.74 (−0.8%) in 28 days; rupees per US dollar, the rupee firmer on the month.

Nothing here is real-time: the oldest prints this note leans on are WPI inflation (20 days); CPI inflation (20 days); Bank credit growth (20 days).
